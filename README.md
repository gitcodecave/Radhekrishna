# Radhekrishna Tapovan Digital Sanctuary

Create a premium, modern and immersive website for “Yugala Radhekrishna Tapovan” using the provided logo and website content.

DESIGN DIRECTION

The UI must be inspired directly by the uploaded logo.

The website should feel:

• Sacred

• Premium

• Devotional

• Warm

• Peaceful

• Culturally rooted

• Contemporary

• Transformational

Avoid making it look like a generic temple, yoga, Ayurveda, wedding or religious-template website. It should combine traditional Radhe-Krishna spirituality with sophisticated modern web design.

COLOR PALETTE — DERIVED FROM LOGO

Primary:

Sacred Crimson — #830202

Deep Maroon — #670101

Saffron Amber — #CF7902

Accents:

Temple Gold — #D39B3E

Antique Gold — #A07039

Divine Light Gold — #E3C477

Neutrals:

Warm Ivory — #F5E8C7

Sacred Brown — #562C10

Deep Shadow — #2C0803

Use gold as the primary accent.

Do NOT make every section red/orange. Balance the website using warm ivory sections for breathing space, alternating with deep crimson, saffron and dark maroon sections.

Use gradients inspired by the logo:

Crimson → Saffron → Gold

Use them selectively rather than everywhere.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use an elegant combination such as:

Headings — Cinzel / Cormorant Garamond

Body & UI — Manrope

Headings should feel elegant and spiritual, while body typography remains modern and highly readable.

VISUAL LANGUAGE

Take subtle inspiration from elements within the logo:

• Sun/light → consciousness and awakening

• Tree/branches → growth

• Radhe & Krishna → love and consciousness

• Intertwined form → Yugala, harmony and union

• Gold → divinity and illumination

• Crimson → divine love and devotion

• Saffron → spiritual energy

Use these concepts subtly through layouts, lines, section transitions and backgrounds.

Do not repeatedly copy the actual logo elements.

UI STYLE

Use:

• Large editorial typography

• Generous whitespace

• Cinematic Radhe-Krishna and nature imagery

• Premium Indian spiritual imagery

• Subtle golden line details

• Minimal sacred patterns

• Soft divine-light effects

• Elegant asymmetrical layouts

• Full-width immersive sections

• Refined image/text compositions

Avoid:

• Excessive rounded cards

• SaaS-style grids

• Glassmorphism everywhere

• Cheap gold effects

• Oversaturated orange

• Excessive religious ornamentation

• Too many icons

• Generic stock imagery

HERO

Create an exceptional cinematic hero.

Headline:

“Where Love Meets Consciousness.”

Supporting:

“A Sacred Space Inspired by Radhe-Krishna for Inner Transformation, Devotion & Conscious Living.”

CTA:

“Begin Your Spiritual Journey”

Use premium Radhe-Krishna/nature imagery with subtle warm golden illumination. The hero should immediately communicate devotion, peace, love and spiritual depth.

IMPORTANT SECTIONS

Use the supplied website content to build sections for:

• About Tapovan

• Meaning of Yugala

• Radhe & Krishna

• Vision & Mission

• Spiritual Philosophy

• Krishna Mandala Experience

• Goloka Vision

• Retreats & Programs

• Gurukulam

• Rasa Leela

• Community

• Global Rasaleela Festival

• Spiritual Store

• Publications

• Founder – Srikrishna Abhirra

• Long-Term Vision

• Yugala Life connection

• Contact

• Footer

KRISHNA MANDALA

Give special UI attention to this section.

Do NOT show the 11 Krishna dimensions as ordinary cards.

Create an elegant mandala-inspired, circular, timeline or scroll-based experience that progresses through the dimensions and culminates in Yugalakrishna.

ANIMATION

Keep animations calm and premium:

• smooth scrolling

• slow fade/reveal

• subtle image parallax

• golden line animations

• soft light movement

• gentle section transitions

No bouncing, flashy effects or aggressive animations.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The website must look intentionally designed on mobile, tablet and desktop—not simply stacked from the desktop version.

FINAL RULE

The website should feel like the digital extension of the Yugala Radhekrishna Tapovan logo.

Create visual variety instead of repeating:

“heading + paragraph + cards.”

The final experience should feel:

Sacred × Premium × Indian × Contemporary × Devotional × Transformational.
